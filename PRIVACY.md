# Privacy

The Reverse Engineering skill does not collect, store, or transmit any data.

- **No network access.** None of the bundled scripts open a network connection.
- **No telemetry or analytics.** Nothing about your usage, your files, or your machine is recorded or sent anywhere.
- **Local only.** The scripts read the files you point them at, on your machine (or in your agent's sandbox), and print results to your agent. They never execute the files being analyzed.
- **No accounts or credentials.** The skill asks for no keys, tokens, or personal information.

Your AI agent (for example Claude) processes the skill's output under that agent's own privacy terms. Any reverse-engineering tools you choose to install and run alongside the skill, such as Ghidra or jadx, follow their own policies.
